# Hi there! I'm an Author and Contemporary Artist 🌌📚

Welcome to my profile. By day, I handle and preserve literature within a rare book library. By night, I channel astrophysics data into independent contemporary art, poetry, and open educational initiatives. In particular, my flagship work bridges the gap between literary preservation and citizen science. Currently, select custom artifacts are slated for orbital spaceflight alongside independent art drops destined for domestic and international Free Little Art Galleries.

<summary><b>📖 FRAMEWORK: THE POSITIVE ZERO PRINCIPLE (Click to expand)</b></summary>
<br />
<div align="left" style="padding: 0 15px; font-size: 14px; line-height: 1.6; border-left: 2px solid #30363d;">

*   **THE MISSION** — We believe that art and education should spark real-world connection through transformative projects. As part of this creative ecosystem, our books are designed to live out in the world—some are tucked away in hidden corners for unexpected finders, while others are available directly for traditional collectors.
*   **TRANSFORMATIVE PROJECTS** — The studio's signature project is a borderless treasure hunt where our literary works are hidden across the earth and launched into space. Grounded in a "positive zero" methodology, the project invites anyone who interacts with the archive to commit a quiet act of kindness. The heart of this initiative was highlighted at the Ely Center for Contemporary Art during our CT Tech Week exhibition and is currently featured on the Free Little Art Galleries website.
*   **EXPERIMENTAL STEM DESIGN** — We design beginner-friendly, hyper-visual curriculum models focused on foundational physics. Our signature lesson, the *10-Second Space-Only Photon Rocket*, challenges students to build an active rocket model using household materials to explore the laws of light propulsion and velocity. The curriculum references the absolute speed limit of the universe to illustrate how an unblocked stream of light particles can travel to the Moon in approximately 1.3 seconds before traversing past the stars forever.
*   **CONCEPTUAL MATHEMATICS** — The project’s core philosophy stems from an ambitious attempt to disprove **Beal’s Conjecture**—a million-dollar math equation that has remained unsolved for decades. By analyzing the concept of "positive zero" to challenge the equation, the findings were presented at the 19th annual CMC3 conference and at MathFest in Washington, D.C., celebrating the 100th anniversary of the Mathematical Association of America.
*   **FOUNDER RECOGNITION** — This mathematical journey earned our founder regional distinction and the Westfair Milli Award for Innovation, serving as the ultimate creative catalyst for launching hidden waves of kindness globally.
*   **APPLIED LITERACY** — Bridging academic mathematics with real-world applications, the studio curates open introductory financial literacy learning tracks. Our founder and core philosophy were featured in a national *New York Times* advertising campaign, highlighting how passive investments can unlock the freedom and time required to relentlessly pursue creative passions. *(Note: Content is a community resource guide for independent study; not formal financial advice).*
